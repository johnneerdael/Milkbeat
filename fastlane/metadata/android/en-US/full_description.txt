Milkbeat is a music-first YouTube Music client for Android TV, built for the remote.

Features:

- Your YouTube Music home on the TV: mood chips and every shelf, in YouTube Music's order
- Sign in with your phone to see your own YouTube Music feeds; playback stays anonymous
- Search that starts on music, with videos one press away for live sets and concerts
- Full-screen now playing with an editable queue
- Artist, album and playlist pages with a mini player across the app
- Background playback on by default
- On-device recommendation engine with no analytics or tracking
- Modern Material 3 interface

Milkbeat is a fork of Flow by A-EDev.
