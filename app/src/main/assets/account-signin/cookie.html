Connect Spotify to Milkbeat

Your Spotify cookie gives access to your account. Before entering it, check that this page’s certificate SHA-256 fingerprint matches your TV. Only use this page on a network you trust.

  1. Open Spotify Web Player in another browser tab and sign in.
  2. Open your browser’s developer tools, then Application (or Storage) → Cookies → https://open.spotify.com.
  3. Find sp_dc and copy its value. Return to this page and paste it below.
